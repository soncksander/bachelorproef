# Beef Tacos and more

**Ground beef is incredibly versatile. Enjoy the tacos below and turn leftovers into completely different meals with the addition of only one or 2 ingredients.**

## Ingredients:

* 1 Tbsp. olive oil

* ½ large yellow onion or 1 medium, finely diced

* 3 or 4 garlic cloves, finely diced

* 1kg ground beef (can use ground chicken if preferred)

* ½ tsp chili powder

* ½ tsp ground cumin

* ½ tsp paprika

* ½ tsp good quality salt

* ¼ tsp crushed red pepper

* Pepper to taste

### Fixings:

* Flour or hard corn taco shells

* Grated cheese

* Diced tomato

* Shredded lettuce or cabbage

* Avocado

* Fresh cilantro etc.

Photo of three beef tacos with fixings

## Directions:

1. Gently heat olive oil

2. Cook onions until soft and starting to carmelize

3. Add garlic, cook another 1 - 2 minutes being careful not to burn

4. Add ground beef breaking it up as it cooks.

5. Once no longer pink drain extra fat and then add all of the spices.

6. Adjust seasonings to taste

– you may want to double the amounts listed and serve in tortilla / taco shell with the fixings as desired.

***

## Revival Meal Ideas:

### 1. Stuffed peppers:
Combine leftover rice with leftover beef taco mix (1:1 ratio) to make tasty stuffed peppers. Try red, yellow or orange….

Simply slice top off of the peppers, remove seeds, fill with rice and beef mixture and place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper (cheap at Costco). Bake at 400 ºF until the peppers are soft but not mushy and the rice/beef mix is heated through (25 minutes will heat everything through and soften the pepper up a bit. 350ºF for 45 minutes is great if you want a softer pepper). Top with grated cheese in the last few minutes if desired

### 2. Taco Grilled Cheese:

Turn oven onto broil. Butter one side of your favorite bread and place in frying fry on medium heat. Put generous portion of taco mix on bread and top with shredded cheese. When bottom of bread is nicely toasted transfer frying pan to oven and broil until cheese is melted. Add second slice of bread under broiler to toast until just browned. when cheese is melted remove from oven top with tomato slices, avocado, lettuce, then top with toasted bread

### 3. Taco Pizza:

Simply top Greek pita with leftover taco mixture, shredded mozzarella, diced tomato, peppers or hot peppers (if desired), fresh spinach and grated fresh lime or lemon peel (optional) and bake pizza as you normally would.
