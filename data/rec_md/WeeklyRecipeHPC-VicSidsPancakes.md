# Sid’s High Protein Pancake

One of the easiest pancake recipes you will ever make and one of the tastiest you will eat. A great source of both high quality protein and carbohydrate: perfect for fueling and recovery.

## When to use:

Pre or post training breakfast, lunch or even dinner. Make extra for an easy, carry along snack.

## Ingredients:

* 250 ml/1 cup old fashioned rolled oats – dry
* 5 eggs
* 250 ml/1 cup 1% cottage cheese - heaping
* 1 medium ripe banana
* 4 tsp./20ml baking powder
* Vanilla or cinnamon to taste (optional)

> **Nutrition Facts Per Recipe:**
> * Calories – 940
> * Carbs – 98 grams
> * Protein – 75 grams
> * Fat – 34 grams
> * Fiber – 11 grams

## Directions

1. Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until thoroughally mixed.
2. Heat pan/griddle and cook as you would regular pancakes flipping when the surface of the pancake bubbles.

## Tips and Toppings:

* Batter thinkens as it sits – thin with a splash of milk if needed.
* Delicious topped with plain yogurt and a sweetener such as, berries, honey or maple syrup.
