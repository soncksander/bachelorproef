# Sues Throw Together Recovery Muesli

**This make a very large batch: 23, ½ cup servings**

A delicious and nutritious way to recover.

Feel free to add more or less of an ingredient or add other items that you may have on hand. Note that this will alter the macronutrient profile. Nutrients listed below are for ½ cup muesli only.

Serve with plain yogurt, Greek yogurt or milk for a protein, carbohydrate, fibre and nutrient rich snack.

Adding ½ - 1 cup of plain yogurt will add an additional 4 – 15 grams of protein depending on if you use regular or Greek so be sure to read the label.

Aim to consume approximately your weight in kilograms as carbs (eg. 65kg x 1 = 65 gram carb) and 20 – 30 grams of protein as soon as possible after swim sessions and 20 plus grams of protein before bed.

## Ingredients:

* 3.5 cups raw, old-fashioned oats

* 3.5 cups pumpkin seeds

* 2 cups dried cranberries

* 1.5 cups tart cherries

* 1 cup slivered almonds

* 0.5 cup raisins

* 0.5 cup brown sugar or coconut sugar

* Cinnamon to taste – several tablespoons or more

Bowl of muesli

Muesli in a glass with fruit

## Directions:

1. Combine ingredients and stir to mix well.

2. Serve with yogurt or milk

3. Eat as recovery, breakfast or bedtime snack

### Other ingredients to consider:

* Chia seeds, sunflower seeds, sesame seeds

* Walnuts, pecans. pistachios

* Dried blueberries

* Toasted coconut flakes or shredded coconut
