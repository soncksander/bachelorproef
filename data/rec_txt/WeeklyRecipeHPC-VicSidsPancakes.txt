   Sid’s High Protein Pancake


    One of the easiest pancake recipes you will ever make and one of the tastiest you will eat.
    A great source of both high quality protein and carbohydrate: perfect for fueling and
    recovery.

    When to use:
    Pre or post training breakfast, lunch or even dinner.  Make extra for an easy, carry along
    snack.

    Ingredients:
    250 ml/1 cup old fashioned rolled oats – dry           Nutrition Facts Per Recipe:
    5 eggs                                                 Calories – 940
    250 ml/1 cup 1% cottage cheese - heaping               Carbs – 98 grams
    1 medium ripe banana                                   Protein – 75 grams
    4 tsp./20ml baking powder                              Fat – 34 grams
    Vanilla or cinnamon to taste (optional)                Fiber – 11 grams

    Directions
    Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until thoroughally mixed.
    Heat pan/griddle and cook as you would regular pancakes flipping when the surface of the
    pancake bubbles.

   Tips and Toppings:
   Batter thinkens as it sits – thin with a splash of milk if needed.
   Delicious topped with plain yogurt and a sweetener such as, berries, honey or maple syrup.
