    Sues Throw Together Recovery Muesli

    This make a very large batch: 23, ½ cup servings

    A delicious and nutritious way to recover.
    Feel free to add more or less of an ingredient or add other items that you may have on hand. Note that this will alter the
    macronutrient profile. Nutrients listed below are for ½ cup muesli only.
    Serve with plain yogurt, Greek yogurt or milk for a protein, carbohydrate, fibre and nutrient rich snack.
    Adding ½ - 1 cup of plain yogurt will add an additional 4 – 15 grams of protein depending on if you use regular or Greek so be
    sure to read the label.
    Aim to consume approximately your weight in kilograms as carbs (eg. 65kg x 1 = 65 gram carb) and 20 – 30 grams of protein
    as soon as possible after swim sessions and 20 plus grams of protein before bed.
