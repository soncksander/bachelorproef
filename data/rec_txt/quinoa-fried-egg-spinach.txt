Quinoa with Fried Egg and
Spinach

Ingredients:

1 cups     cooked quinoa, ½ cup dry
2 cup      baby spinach
1-2        eggs
Cooking spray, soy sauce, sesame oil, Sriracha sauce
(Vietnamese chilli sauce) to taste.


Making it:

1.     Measure ½ cup (or more) of quinoa. Add 1 cup water and simmer for 15 min. Take lid off and fluff
       with fork. If you use more quinoa the ratio of quinoa to water is 1:2.
2.     Frying pan: add ½ cup of water and spinach. Cover with a lid to lightly steam spinach. If you have a
       steamer you can also use that as well. Even quicker toss spinach it in the microwave with a little bit
       of water, covered with lid slightly open (~ 1 – 2 min.)
3.     Small flying pan: Spray cooking oil. Break egg(s) onto hot pan and lightly fry, flipping once.
4.     Layer quinoa and egg on top of spinach and flavor with soy, sesame oil and Sriracha’s to taste.
